# BookFold Dual-Licensing Model

BookFold is available under two licenses:

| | **AGPL-3.0-or-later** (default) | **Commercial License** |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | Paid — see [COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md) |
| Private use, family, study | ✅ | — |
| Internal business use | ✅ | — |
| Modifying and self-hosting | ✅ (source must be offered to users) | ✅ without AGPL source-offering duty |
| Distributing your own app that embeds BookFold | ✅ only if your entire app is AGPL-licensed | ✅ your app stays proprietary |
| Embedding/redistribution in commercial products (OEM) | ❌ | ✅ |
| Support | Community | Optional, included in commercial tiers |

## Why dual licensing?

- **Users first:** everyone can use, study, modify and share BookFold freely
  (AGPL). The app itself is free — including the App Store build.
- **Sustainability:** selling commercial licenses funds development, so the
  project does not depend on data harvesting or lock-in.
- **Fairness:** companies profiting from embedding BookFold in proprietary
  products contribute back — either by opening up their code (AGPL) or by
  buying a license.

## How it works legally

- The repository is licensed under `AGPL-3.0-or-later` (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
- The project owner holds copyright and — thanks to the
  [CLA](.cla/CLA-INDIVIDUAL.md) signed by every contributor — the exclusive
  right to additionally offer the same code under commercial terms.
- **You do not need a commercial license if** you only use BookFold privately,
  internally in your organization, or distribute software that fully complies
  with the AGPL (including offering your source code to users).
- **You need a commercial license if** you distribute BookFold (or derivatives)
  inside proprietary software, or offer it as part of a closed SaaS without
  fulfilling AGPL obligations.
