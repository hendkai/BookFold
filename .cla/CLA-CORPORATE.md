An authorized representative signs electronically by replying to the CLA
Assistant bot's comment on the Corporation's pull request with:

`signatures/version1/cla.json`). By signing, the representative confirms the
authorization described in section 3. If the Corporation requires a signed
PDF instead, contact snailberg@kaiser-mail.de.
