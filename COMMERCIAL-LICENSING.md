# BookFold Commercial Licensing

Using BookFold inside proprietary software, or distributing it commercially,
requires a commercial license (see [DUAL-LICENSING.md](DUAL-LICENSING.md)).
Using the app itself — privately or inside your organization — is free under
the AGPL.

## What a commercial license gives you

- The right to embed, modify and distribute BookFold within proprietary
  products, without the AGPL obligations (no requirement to open-source your
  product or offer source to users).
- The right to sub-license to your customers where needed for distribution.
- Updates and fixes of versions released during the license period.

## FAQ

**Do I need a license for private or company-internal use?**
No. AGPL covers that completely, for free.

**Do I need a license because the app is on the App Store?**
No. The BookFold app itself is free; the App Store build is AGPL-licensed by
its owner.

**We ship an open-source (AGPL-compatible) app that embeds BookFold — do we
need a license?**
No, provided you comply with the AGPL (offer source, keep notices intact).

**We offer a closed SaaS that uses BookFold server-side?**
That is distribution/interaction over a network — either fulfill the AGPL
(source offering) or get an OEM license.

**Can we get a custom perpetual license?**
Yes — ask. One-time OEM terms are the standard starting point.
